## Decisions
_Spec reviewed 2026-06-14 via /pm:spec-review. Verdict: approve._

- **[decision] Default-prompt strategy** — One adaptive default prompt, not two
  mode-specific variants. A single editable `DEFAULT_SYSTEM_PROMPT` that answers
  general legal questions from the model's own knowledge and grounds claims in
  documents when they're attached. Preserves the one-visible-editable-template
  transparency model (no mode-switching logic).
- **[decision] Empty-docs sentinel in `renderDocuments`** — When no documents are
  selected, render an explicit `(No documents attached — answer from general
  legal knowledge.)` sentinel instead of an empty string, so the model
  unambiguously sees the no-doc mode.
- **[decision] Confidence redefinition for no-doc mode in `CITATION_CORE`** — When
  no documents are attached, `confidence` means the strength of the general legal
  answer (not document support), and the `citations` array is empty.
- **[assumption] Composer placeholder** — "Ask a legal question, or about the
  selected documents…". Confirmed.
- **[assumption] Message-thread empty state** — heading "Ask a legal question" /
  hint "Ask anything in law, or select documents in the sidebar to ground answers
  in them." Confirmed.
- **[assumption] Context-strip empty hint** — "no documents selected — answering
  from general legal knowledge" (neutral, not a warning). Confirmed.
- **[risk] Footer protocol must not break** — the chat route's
  `parseAssistantFooter` requires the citation footer on every answer; degrade
  only the citation *wording* (conditional on docs), never stop emitting the
  footer. Acknowledged.
- **[risk] Sequencing with #18** — #18 just touched `message-thread.tsx` and
  `context-strip.tsx`; the worktree forks from latest `main`, which already
  includes #18, so #23 edits #18's final wording. Acknowledged.
- **[context] Scope fence to #24** — tools / web-search are out of scope; this is
  copy + prompt wording only, no capability change. Noted.
- **[context] Transparency model unchanged** — the editable template stays the
  single visible default; the strategy block stays the appended, non-editable
  footer. Noted.

> **Additional (out of scope for #23, captured for later):** When the adaptive
> prompt replaces the current "contract review assistant" default, **preserve the
> old contract-review text in an archive** (e.g. a named constant) rather than
> deleting it — it becomes the first entry for a future **template switcher**:
> role-based system-prompt templates plus user-created templates. The switcher
> itself is a separate future task, not part of #23.
